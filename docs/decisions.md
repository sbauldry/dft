| 2026-10-04 | Cognitive impairment = **dementia only** (Langa-Weir `cogfunction == 3`) in the main analysis. CIND + dementia (`cogfunction %in% 2:3`) is a planned **sensitivity analysis**. | Consistent with the authors' previous work. Tradeoff acknowledged: states involving cognition are sparse and cognitive recovery is rare; dementia group relies more on proxy classification (10–15% of dementia cases vs 2–7% of respondents overall). |
| 2026-10-04 | Physical impairment = **≥1 difficulty on the RAND 5-item ADL summary** (`r#adl5a > 0`: walk, dress, bathe, eat, bed). Functional = 0 ADL difficulties. IADLs and mobility excluded. | Consistent with the authors' previous work and with the Lynch & Zang illustration; keeps physical measurement separate from cognition. Sensitivity analyses (e.g., ADL ≥2, 6-item set) deferred; user will decide later. |
| 2026-10-04 | Analytic sample: (a) age **50+, applied per interval** (age at start of interval ≥50; younger spouses enter once they turn 50); (b) include all cohorts that contribute ≥1 interval (original cohorts + Early/Mid/Late Boomer refreshers; entry at first interview; late-entering spouses included); (c) **keep all household members** (no one-per-household restriction); (d) **do not exclude foreign-born**. | Age 50+ matches Lynch & Zang and retains onset at 50–64. Keeping all members avoids dropping ~1/3 of the 1998 sample given sparse cells under the dementia-only cut; household clustering noted as a limitation (one-per-household possible sensitivity). No regional covariates, so no reason to exclude foreign-born. |
| 2026-10-04 | Missing waves / nonresponse / attrition: only consecutive-wave intervals with both start and end states observed, plus intervals ending in death (`iwstat == 5` at next wave). A skipped wave censors the person at the last response; a return after a gap re-enters as a new start (2-year spacing preserved, no bridging). Deaths occurring after a missed wave are ignored (censored at last response), consistent with the `iwstat == 5` mortality rule. Non-death attrition censored at last response. Intervals with a missing start or end state are dropped (<0.5%). | Matches Lynch & Zang (censor attrition, drop missing transitions) and keeps equal 2-year intervals. Limitation: informative attrition (sicker respondents drop out; their later deaths are not counted) biases toward healthier survivors; attrition-weight sensitivity possible later. Counts: 4,278 returns after one skipped wave; 1,825 deaths after ≥1 missed wave; 8,737 alive/unobserved at last response. |
| 2026-10-04 | Covariates (starting set): **age** (linear + quadratic, age at start of interval), **sex**, **race/ethnicity** (NH White ref, NH Black, Hispanic, NH other), **education** (RAND `raeduc`: <HS ref, HS/GED, some college, college+; ~1% missing dropped), **birth cohort** (linear, centered). Excluded for now: marital status, wealth/income, nativity, region, interactions. Natural spline for age to be considered later. Analysis may be **stratified by sex** (to be decided later; sex would then drop out as a covariate). | Matches the authors' past work (linear + quadratic age). Time-varying and likely-mediating covariates (marital status, wealth) are excluded to keep life-table covariate profiles simple. Age, period and cohort are collinear over 1998–2022, so cohort effects also absorb period trends (incl. 2020); describe as cohort/period in write-up. |
| 2026-10-04 | Transition interval: **treat every interval as exactly 2 years** in the main analysis (Lynch & Zang approach). **Sensitivity analysis:** add interval length (months between interview end dates, centered at 24) as a covariate and fix at 24 months for life tables. | Matches the article and the equal-spacing rule in the missing-wave decision. Observed spacing: median 24 months, 5th–95th percentile 17–31, only 40% within 22–26; median varies by wave pair 21–28 months. Assumption that width is unrelated to covariates is untested; the sensitivity analysis addresses it. |
